  - question: What is MIT Learn?
    answer: |
      MIT Learn is the central destination for MIT’s growing collection of non-degree learning experiences, designed to help you build in-demand skills, engage your curiosity, explore emerging technologies, and advance your goals at your own pace.

      The platform brings together a wide range of learning opportunities, including MIT-designed courses, videos, podcasts, and other learning resources across nearly every field. While more than 98% of the content is free and open to learners worldwide, you’ll also find professional certificates and advanced programs. Access requirements and learning experiences vary by offering, with some available without an account and others requiring you to sign in or create an account.

      AskTIM, MIT Learn’s AI assistant, helps you discover content aligned with your interests. Across a growing range of MIT offerings, it can summarize lessons, reinforce key concepts, and support your learning as you progress, with available features varying by offering and access requirements.
  - question: Why is OpenCourseWare now on MIT Learn?
    answer: |
      Bringing OpenCourseWare to MIT Learn is part of our ongoing commitment to innovation and expanding access to knowledge. By bringing learning content onto MIT Learn, we can introduce new tools like learning paths, AskTIM, and translations that benefit everyone in our community.

      Consolidating our resources also makes it easier for you to discover learning opportunities, explore topics of interest, and navigate MIT’s growing collection of educational content. This move reflects our long-term vision of making educational content more accessible to learners around the world.
  - question: Will MIT OpenCourseWare content or resources change when on MIT Learn?
    answer: |
      No significant changes to OpenCourseWare content or resources are expected. However, you may notice differences in how you navigate the platform, discover content, and view search results. OCW content will also appear alongside other learning opportunities from across MIT, making it easier to explore a broader range of offerings in one place. MIT Learn will continue to add new open and free learning content.
  - question: Will the Creative Commons license change now that OpenCourseWare is on MIT Learn?
    answer: |
      No. OpenCourseWare’s materials will continue to be openly licensed under Creative Commons licenses. Applicable license information and other terms of use will remain available with the content.
  - question: Why should I create a free account on MIT Learn if I can access OpenCourseWare materials without one?
    answer: |
      A free MIT Learn account helps you make the most of your learning experience. You can follow topics that interest you, save resources to revisit later, and receive updates about new learning opportunities as they become available. Creating an account enhances your MIT Learn experience, but you can continue to access OpenCourseWare materials without one.
  - question: What do I need to create an account on MIT Learn?
    answer: |
      You’ll need your full name and a verifiable email address to create an MIT Learn account.
